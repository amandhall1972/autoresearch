The v2 strategy

TriggerEMA, VWAP
Trade only on days when the NIFTY 50 closes above its 100-day EMA and more than 60% of the members in their own uptrend (close above their 100-day EMA) close below their 10-day VWAP: a broad pullback inside a long uptrend.
Name filterEMA, RSI
On a trigger day, a member in its own uptrend whose Wilder RSI(3) is below 20: it was sold hard.
SelectionVWAP
Free slots (12, each 1/12 of the book) go to the eligible names deepest below their 10-day VWAP.
ExitVWAP
Sell as soon as the name closes back above its 10-day VWAP, or after 20 sessions; names leaving the index are sold by the harness.
Execution
Signals on the close, orders at the next open, 15 bps one-way cost (STT, fees, slippage); idle capital earns the RBI repo rate.

Parameters: {"trend_n": 100, "breadth_cut": 0.6, "rsi_n": 3, "rsi_max": 20.0, "vwap_n": 10, "max_hold": 20, "slots": 12}. The source is nse/strategy.py; the research note RCG-RN-REV-002 explains each rule and traces a trigger day to its exits.

How the research ran

  • One objective for every regime: the lowest of the three window Sharpe ratios, so a rule that buys one regime at the cost of another does not raise it.
  • Keep gates against the incumbent: no window more than 0.10 worse, the 10 best days removed, every single calendar year removed, positive years, 0.05 of objective per added parameter, ±20% parameter plateaus, and RSI, EMA and VWAP each measurably used.
  • Researchers worked in parallel with one lens each; every candidate was re-run and audited by an independent agent before the lead decided.
  • Two amendments and one merge were written into program.md before the next round, and before any look at 2011–2016.

Bottom line

2024–2026 is now positive (0.85) and 2020–2026 strong (1.02), but both windows were used to choose the rules. The evidence that was not is the deep hold-out (0.68), the walk-forward re-selection and the joint parameter moves. The strategy is a low-exposure liquidity-provision sleeve, sensitive to costs and to a few rebound days. Paper-trade it with the kill rules of RCG-RN-REV-002 fixed before the first trade.
