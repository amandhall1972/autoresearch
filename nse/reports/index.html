Sealed hold-out, April 2017 – December 2019

Point-in-time Nifty 50 of those years, including 11 names that left the index before 2020. Scored once, after the research ended. The strategy grew 2.1% a year and NIFTYBEES 12.7%, while cash earned 6.4%; Sharpe is measured over cash, so the strategy's is negative.

The strategy

Regime
Trade only while the NIFTY 50's RSI(14) is bullish: switch on above 57, go fully to cash below 53, and stand aside while it is above 80.
Entry (EMA)
EMA20 above EMA50, but not more than 6% above it.
Entry (VWAP)
Close above the 20-day VWAP.
Entry (RSI)
EMA3-smoothed RSI(14) below 70, with no reading above 80 in the last 20 days; the name's volatility expansion (vol20/vol120) is not in the members' top third.
Cohort
One basket is bought on the day the regime switches on and then left to ride: no rebalancing, no replacement buys. Weights are single-index mean-variance (shrunk beta over stock-specific variance), tilted to the strongest 3-day VWAP position and away from names with a recent shock or volume spike; no name above 40%, the excess parked in NIFTYBEES.
Re-entry
After a stand-aside at RSI 80, the book re-enters with the 5 names that score highest on VWAP20/VWAP50 momentum times RSI strength relative to the index.
Sizing
A fresh basket is scaled down when the Bank Nifty has just become more volatile than usual (20- vs 120-day vol), and bought at 50% after a whipsaw of 3 days or fewer.
While held
From day 3 the bottom 33% of the book by its own score is sold once, and the book is trimmed to a 10% volatility budget, least-liked names first. When market breadth (share of members above VWAP20) dips more than 15 points below its average and turns up, the surviving names are topped up with idle cash within that budget.
Exits
A name is sold on a close below 0.985 × its 20-day VWAP, when its 3-day VWAP drops under its 50-day VWAP, on a stock-specific shock (2.5 residual sigmas) or a 4× volume spike. Once 60% of the basket has been sold the rest goes too, and everything is sold when the regime switches off.
Execution
Signals on the close, orders at the next open, 15 bps one-way cost (STT, fees, slippage), idle cash earns the RBI repo rate.

Multiple testing. The deflated Sharpe ratio over 3,419 logged trials is 0.996 (hurdle 4.08). That calculation treats the trials as independent draws; a search that keeps building on its own best result on the same four years is not that, so read it as a check on the arithmetic of selection, not as evidence of an edge. The out-of-sample columns above are the better guide.

Parameters. 144 one-at-a-time ±10/20% perturbations: median in-sample Sharpe 4.96 (base 5.09), 62% within 0.2 of the base; median 2024–26 Sharpe -1.06. Walk-forward re-selection of those parameters year by year gives a Sharpe of 1.91 over 2021–2026, mostly from 2021–2023, which the grid's centre was tuned on; the 2024–2026 test years are negative.

How the research ran

  • One editable file (strategy.py), a fixed harness, one metric: the 2020–2023 Sharpe. Up to six parallel researchers per round (four in round 8, when two failed to start), each in its own git worktree, each with one lens; the lead verified, merged and combined their winners.
  • Experiments per round: round 1: 245, round 2: 916, round 3: 502, round 4: 372, round 5: 370, round 6: 482, round 7: 312, round 8: 226.
  • A keep had to raise in-sample Sharpe and pass five gates: both halves of 2020–2023, the five best days removed, every new parameter on a plateau, simplifications allowed, and no single trading episode carrying the gain.
  • After round 1 the lead looked at the 2024–2026 numbers, so that window became a validation window and a fresh, sealed 2017–2019 hold-out was built before round 2. From round 2 researchers saw neither.
  • Every run passes a dense lookahead check: weights are recomputed on truncated and future-scrambled data and must not change.

Bottom line

The loop did what it was asked: it found the rules that best explain 2020–2023. Those years held a crash and a strong recovery that a regime-timed, high-conviction book could exploit. After 2023 the same rules lose to cash: Sharpe -1.06 over 2024–2026, while the Nifty itself managed -0.28. Treat the in-sample figure as a measure of fit, not of expected return. Before trading any version, paper-trade it and judge it on data the loop has not seen.

Reproduce

cd nse
python run.py                    # in-sample metrics + lookahead check (~10 s)
python run.py --report           # adds 2024–26 and full-period metrics
python validate.py --boot 2000   # costs, bootstrap, deflated Sharpe, perturbations, walk-forward
python lineage.py --holdout      # every kept strategy on all windows (logs each hold-out look)
python signals.py --capital 1000000 --holdings my_holdings.csv   # tomorrow's orders
